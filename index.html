⚡ Engineering Philosophy

Think Beyond Design. Engineer for Performance.

Great engineering is not limited to creating a 3D model. It begins with understanding the problem, evaluating design requirements, applying appropriate engineering principles, and developing solutions that address real-world industrial challenges.

My approach combines concept development, mechanical architecture, detailed 3D CAD design, engineering calculations, structural evaluation, and manufacturing considerations to develop functional and technically informed engineering solutions.

I believe effective mechanical design must consider more than geometry. It must account for component functionality, system integration, structural requirements, manufacturability, assembly, reliability, and the practical conditions in which the design will operate.

Lightweight E-Axle Housing Design & Optimization for Electric Vehicles

Independent Engineering Development Concept | Research & Technical Study

An independent engineering development concept focused on lightweight e-axle housing architecture for electric vehicle applications. The study explores mechanical design methodology, structural loading considerations, engineering calculations, finite element analysis workflows, and topology optimization approaches.

The objective is to investigate how engineering-driven design decisions, structural evaluation methodologies, and manufacturing considerations can contribute to a mechanically suitable, weight-conscious housing concept while addressing defined structural design requirements.

HyperMesh ANSA NX Nastran Autodesk Nastran

Key Engineering Development Areas

  • E-Axle Housing Architecture: Exploring housing configuration, mechanical architecture, component arrangement, and CAD-based design development.
  • Structural Loading & Torque Reactions: Identifying relevant load cases, torque-reaction considerations, mounting interfaces, and bearing-support regions.
  • Engineering Calculations: Establishing calculation methods and design considerations for evaluating mechanical and structural requirements.
  • Finite Element Analysis (FEA): Studying analysis preparation, boundary-condition considerations, and structural evaluation workflows.
  • Mesh Preparation & Quality: Exploring finite element model preparation, mesh quality assessment, and analysis readiness using HyperMesh and ANSA.
  • Structural Analysis Workflow: Reviewing solver-based analysis methodologies associated with NX Nastran and Autodesk Nastran.
  • Topology & Lightweight Optimization: Investigating material distribution, rib-architecture concepts, and topology-optimization approaches for potential mass reduction.
  • Manufacturing & Design Feasibility: Considering manufacturability, assembly access, interfaces, and practical housing-design constraints.
  • Technical Documentation & Innovation Research: Organizing design rationale, engineering methods, technical descriptions, and patent-oriented research material.
